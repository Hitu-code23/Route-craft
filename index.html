          Ask a consultant

          If your customers, shop or government contracts are inside the UAE, mainland usually makes sense. If you mainly serve international or online clients, a free zone is often faster and more cost-effective. Our Route Planner gives you a first answer in 30 seconds, and a consultant confirms it for free.

          Yes, in most cases. Free zone and offshore companies are fully foreign-owned, and most mainland activities now allow 100% foreign ownership too. A small number of strategic activities still need an Emirati partner or service agent, and we’ll flag that upfront.

          Many free zone licences are issued within a few working days once documents are complete. Mainland setups and regulated activities such as healthcare, finance and crypto take longer because of extra approvals. Visas and bank accounts follow the licence.

          Most of the licence process can be done remotely. You’ll usually need to be in the country for your visa medical, Emirates ID biometrics and, in most cases, the bank account meeting. We plan your visit so it’s short and efficient.

          It depends on your jurisdiction, package and office space. In free zones the visa quota comes with your package, while on the mainland it’s tied to the size of your office. Offshore companies aren’t eligible for visas.

          Most UAE businesses need to register for corporate tax, and VAT registration is mandatory once you cross the turnover threshold. Our accounting team handles registrations, bookkeeping and filings so you stay compliant with the FTA.

          Yes, and this is where most of our relationships really start. Licence renewals, amendments, PRO work, new visas, VAT, accounting and audits are all handled by the same team that set you up.
